## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing ApnoMart's sales data to understand business performance, profitability, regional sales, and customer purchasing patterns. I created an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore the data, identify important trends, and present useful insights that can support business decisions.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Sales Performance:** Furniture recorded the highest sales among product categories, at approximately INR 35.18 lakh. The East region had the highest regional sales, at approximately INR 34.37 lakh. July recorded the highest monthly sales, at around INR 2.96 million.

2. **Profitability:** Office Supplies generated the highest category profit, approximately INR 11.77 lakh, and had the highest profit margin at 34.81%. Electronics had the lowest category profit margin at 18.88%.

3. **Customer Insights:** Corporate customers contributed approximately INR 4.31 million in sales. Cash was the most frequently used payment method, with 258 orders.

## Strategic Recommendation

Based on my analysis, I recommend focusing on retaining Corporate customers and continuing to support the strong performance of Office Supplies. The business should also review Electronics pricing, product costs, and discount strategies to improve its profit margin. Sales performance in the East region and during July can be studied further to identify opportunities for future growth.
